About Me

Art since day one.
Ink is next.

I'm a young artist working toward becoming a tattoo artist — and I'm taking on the challenge of building a small business around my own products and supplies. I've been making art since I was young and kept at it through the years.

I've competed in art competitions, seen my mistakes up close, and improved on every one of them. What I want most is for people to be truly satisfied with tattoos designed and made by me — and to keep that love going by supporting the brand.

If the art keeps spreading, the dream is to open my own tattoo trade school for people who want to become tattoo artists and start their own journey.
