      About the founder

      Meet Cameron.

      I'm Cameron, a 16-year-old high school junior and the founder of Kingdom Made.

      It started in a classroom. A teacher I'm grateful for showed me how important it is to build your own brand: to know what you stand for and let it show in everything you create. I knew right away what I wanted mine to stand for.

      More of Him. Less of me. Those words are the heart of everything we make. They mean putting Jesus above myself, and living in a way that brings glory to God instead of to me.

      I design clothing that represents Jesus because a shirt can start a conversation, and that conversation might be exactly what someone needs to hear. The tech is the fun side: everyday gear that's simply cool to own, with faith built in.

      “I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me.” Philippians 4:13

      Cameron Founder, Kingdom Made
